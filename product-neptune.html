Neptune

The AT-IPIC water intelligence platform — water balance, treatment selection, and cost-of-value analysis in one decision tool.

What It Does

Water Decisions, Grounded in Data

Neptune models water balance across a site, evaluates treatment options against cost and performance, and translates the result into a clear investment and sustainability case — replacing scattered engineering estimates with one consistent analytical tool.

Built for industrial water managers, Neptune connects operational reality — flows, quality, and treatment capacity — directly to the financial and value-creation metrics that decisions actually get made on.

💧

Streamlit-Powered Analysis

Neptune runs as a dedicated Streamlit application with its own secure login and Neon database, hosted on its own AT-IPIC subdomain.

The Advantage

Why Teams Use Neptune

🌊

Full Water Balance

Models inflows, treatment stages, and outflows together, so the trade-offs of any change are visible before it's made.

⚙️

Treatment Selection

Compares treatment technologies against cost, capacity, and outcome — turning technical options into a comparable decision set.

💰

Value & Cost Modelling

Translates engineering choices into financial and sustainability value-creation metrics leadership can act on directly.
