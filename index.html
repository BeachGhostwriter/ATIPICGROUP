Interactive Demo · Planetary Performance

From one action to planetary impact and EBITDA

Every action captured by the 7 Modules is mapped to one of the nine planetary boundaries, tracked by one KPI and valued in euros. Click a boundary name, a value-driver tab or a wedge to see how the system reports it, down to the individual action.

Within target Target to sector median Worse than sector median FY2024 position

Outer ring: boundary (layer 1) · inner tabs: value driver (layer 2) · wedges: action (layer 3)

How to read the wheel

Each wedge is one action. The green core is the client’s target, the yellow band runs from target to the sector median, and red means worse than the sector median. The shorter the wedge, the closer it is to target.

Click any part of the wheel. Everything else goes out of focus, the EBITDA bridge filters to your selection and a panel explains what you are seeing.

EBITDA bridge

Axis starts at €37.4m. In-progress actions count at half their annual value in FY2025; planned actions add nothing yet.
