One Pager

A single-page format for capturing the actions and root causes identified immediately after an operational incident.

What It Is

Turning Every Incident Into a Documented Improvement

The One Pager captures actions identified following an incident, used in industrial controlling to quantify leakage precisely. Unlike the proactive Value Plan, the One Pager is reactive in origin — but systematic in capture.

Each incident, however small, is recorded in the same structured format. That consistency is what makes it possible to compare losses across sites, functions and time periods — and to see patterns that a one-off incident report never would.

The Three Sources of Value Loss

🔴 Value Leakage

Gains fail to materialise due to reliability issues, quality problems, and operational instability.

🟡 Cognitive Bias

Actions are systematically overestimated while leakage is underestimated — a natural consequence of working inside your own system.

🟠 Execution Gaps

The bridge between "planned" and "achieved" is where most value quietly disappears.

The One Pager feeds Module 4 · Stabilising Production & Capturing Actions and Process 05 · Insight — democratising performance data so production and operations teams get the transparency they need to act.
