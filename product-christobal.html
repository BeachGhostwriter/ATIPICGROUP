Christobal

The AT-IPIC risk & performance assessment platform — geographic asset intelligence for portfolio-wide decision making.

What It Does

Portfolio Risk, Mapped and Ranked

Christobal gives you a single geographic view of every site, asset, or client relationship in your portfolio — dashboards, recommendations, ratings, and vulnerability analysis layered directly onto an interactive, rotating map view.

Instead of scattered spreadsheets and static reports, Christobal keeps risk and performance data current, comparable, and visual — so decision-makers can see where exposure is concentrated and where attention is genuinely needed.

Why Teams Use Christobal
